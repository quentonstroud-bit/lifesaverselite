01Who this policy covers

This policy applies to:

  • Members: people who apply to join or take part in our referral network.
  • Referred individuals: people whose contact details a member submits so a licensed agent can reach out.
  • Visitors: anyone who browses this website.

The website and platform are operated by LifeSavers Elite.

02Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Application details: full name, email address, phone number and current insurance carrier.
  • Account and payout details needed to pay referral bonuses, such as payment account information and tax information (for example, a W-9 form) where required by law.
  • Messages you send us.

Information about people you refer

When you submit a referral, you give us that person’s name, phone number, email address and the type of coverage they may need.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we and our service providers receive basic technical information when you visit, such as your browser type, device type, pages viewed and approximate location derived from your IP address. We use privacy-friendly analytics that do not track you across other websites.

03How we use information

  • To review applications and manage member accounts.
  • To pass referrals to licensed insurance agents so they can contact the referred person.
  • To track referral status in real time and calculate and send payouts.
  • To communicate with you about your account, referrals and payouts.
  • To protect our network, prevent fraud and comply with legal and tax obligations.
  • To improve our website and services.

04Calls, texts and consent

Referred individuals may be contacted by a licensed agent by phone, text or email about insurance coverage. By submitting a referral, you confirm that the person knows you are sharing their details and has agreed to be contacted.

Anyone contacted through our network can opt out at any time by telling the agent, replying STOP to a text, or contacting us. Message and data rates may apply. Consent to be contacted is never a condition of buying any product.

05How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as follows:

  • Licensed insurance agents and agencies in our network, so they can contact referred individuals.
  • Service providers who help us run the website and platform, such as hosting, form processing, analytics and payment processing. They may use the information only to provide those services to us.
  • Legal and safety reasons: when required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our members, the public or LifeSavers Elite.
  • Business transfers: as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

06Cookies and similar technologies

This website uses only the cookies and similar technologies needed to load the site securely and measure overall traffic. We do not use advertising cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work.

07How long we keep information

We keep information for as long as your account is active and as long as needed to process referrals and payouts, resolve disputes, and meet legal, tax and accounting requirements. After that, we delete it or make it anonymous.

08How we protect information

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

09Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your information.
  • Opt out of marketing messages and of being contacted about a referral.
  • Not be treated differently for using these rights.

To make a request, email us at info@lifesaverselite.com or call +1 (760) 681-4948. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

10Children

Our services are for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date above, and for significant changes we will give notice on this website or by email.
